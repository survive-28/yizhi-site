The company

Smart care for
graceful aging.

YIZHI Smart Care Technology (Suzhou) Co., Ltd. is an export-focused smart-care company built around one question: what does a family actually need to know about a single dose?

We are the authorized export and OEM partner of Suzhou Xiangshi Information Technology Co., Ltd., and the company behind the Healthcare Nurse W100. From Suzhou, we deliver the product end to end — multilingual localization, certification, retail packaging, logistics and after-sales — to distributors, pharmacy chains and DTC channels across Europe, North America and Southeast Asia.

The W100 was co-developed with a tertiary hospital, and its invention patent is co-owned by that hospital. Six sensors, voice prompts and a closed loop of remind–open–record–report turn "remembering" into evidence of care.

We build medication-adherence support and care assistance — never diagnosis, treatment or efficacy claims. Certifications in progress: CE / RoHS / FDA registration.

The product

Healthcare Nurse W100

Six sealed compartments, two layers of three. It is set up with a sentence — no smartphone, no network, nothing new to learn.

Six things it does

All-round remindersLight, voice and vibration arrive together.
Anti-miss, anti-doubleSix sensors and on-device logic; the wrong compartment is called out.
Medication logEvery medicine, every dose — recorded automatically.
Family remote alertsTaken, missed or wrong — the family is told.
Food-grade materialsFood-grade ABS and silicone seal out moisture and oxygen.
6 independent compartmentsTwo layers, three each — a week of doses prepared once.
Set up in one sentence

“Set the 7 a.m. dose.” Voice setup, no phone or network required.

OTA updates

Firmware updates over the air — no return, no replacement.

Child-lock

A safety lock keeps small hands out.

Multi-user, multi-device

One carer can look after several elders; siblings can share the watch.
