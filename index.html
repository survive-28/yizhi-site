The company

Smart care for
graceful aging.

YIZHI Smart Care Technology (Suzhou) Co., Ltd. is an export-focused smart-care company built around one question: what does a family actually need to know about a single dose?

We are the authorized export and brand partner of Suzhou Xiangshi Information Technology Co., Ltd., and the company behind the Healthcare Nurse W100. From Suzhou via FOB Shanghai, we deliver end-to-end readiness — multilingual localization, EU conformity dossiers, retail packing, logistics and after-sales SLA — to distributors, pharmacy chains and care institutions across Europe and Southeast Asia.

The W100 was co-developed with a tertiary hospital, and its invention patent is co-owned with that hospital. Six independent opening sensors, voice cues and a closed loop of remind–open–record–report turn remembering into visible peace of mind.

Compliance boundary: The W100 is a non-medical medication-adherence aid per EU MDR (EU) 2017/745 Art. 2(1) — never diagnosis, treatment or proof of actual ingestion. Our 12-document EU conformity dossier (CE Module A, RED/EN 18031, EMC, RoHS, LFGB, GPSR & German EPR) is compiled as a complete trade-fair simulation pack (no FDA claims).
